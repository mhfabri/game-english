◈   TIME AGENT'S GRAMMAR GUIDE

WILL

Use it for spontaneous decisions, promises and opinions or predictions without present evidence.

Example: “I'll help you!”

BE GOING TO

Use it for plans already decided and predictions based on present evidence.

Example: “Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.”

TIME AGENT TIP
Ask yourself: Is it a decision made right now, or a plan/evidence that already exists?
